# AI Agents Championship — Problem-to-Opportunity Framework

A practical framework for turning a real-world problem into a testable AI agent opportunity.

> **Key takeaway:** Validate the problem and the workflow before deciding to build an AI agent.

---

## 1. The Five-Step Framework

### 01 — SPOT: Find the Problem

Identify a recurring, meaningful problem worth solving.

**Ask:**
- Who experiences this problem?
- How often do they experience it?
- What does it cost them?

---

### 02 — MAP: Understand the Current Workflow

Document how the work happens today, including people, tools, data, decisions, and handoffs.

**Ask:**
- Where are the bottlenecks?
- Where are the repetitive tasks?
- Where are the failure points?

---

### 03 — QUALIFY: Test the Opportunity

Evaluate value, feasibility, reliability, human oversight, errors and risks, and whether a simpler solution exists.

**Ask:**
- Does this genuinely need an AI agent?
- Or would a rule, script, or conventional application work better?

---

### 04 — CHOOSE: Prioritise One Opportunity

Compare candidate opportunities and select the one that deserves investment.

**Ask:**
- Which option delivers the clearest measurable benefit?
- At an acceptable level of risk?

---

### 05 — FRAME: Define a Testable Hypothesis

Turn the selected opportunity into a specific experiment with success criteria and a next test.

**Ask:**
- What evidence would convince us that this solution works?

---

## 2. The Opportunity Sheet

The framework proposes a lightweight canvas for documenting the idea — a useful alternative to writing a full product requirements document before you've established whether the opportunity is worth pursuing.

| Component | What to Capture |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **User & Problem** | Who has the problem and what pain they experience |
| **Workflow Map** | Current process, systems, handoffs and decisions |
| **Human Control** | Approval points, escalation and intervention |
| **Choice** | The selected solution and why it is preferable |
| **Evidence** | Baseline metrics, research and supporting observations |
| **Next Test** | The smallest experiment that can validate the hypothesis |

---

## How to Use

1. **SPOT** a recurring, costly problem experienced by a specific user.
2. **MAP** the current end-to-end workflow, noting people, tools, data, decisions, and handoffs.
3. **QUALIFY** the opportunity — confirm an AI agent is the right tool rather than a rule or script.
4. **CHOOSE** the single opportunity with the clearest measurable benefit at acceptable risk.
5. **FRAME** it as a testable hypothesis with defined success criteria and a next experiment.
6. Document everything on the **Opportunity Sheet** before writing any code.
